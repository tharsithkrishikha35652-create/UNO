Open the downloaded file. If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source".

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Android say "unknown source"?
Android classifies any app not installed through the Play Store as an "unknown source". CalCredit is built by Anna University students for students, and is 100% free with no ads.
Does CalCredit request sensitive permissions?
No. CalCredit requires only Internet access to synchronize your academic profile with Firebase. It does not access your camera, contacts, SMS, files, or location.
Can I update the app later without losing marks?
Yes. Every release is signed with the permanent Team Calcredit release key. Future updates will install smoothly over your existing app.
