Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Android ask to allow unknown apps?
Android requires explicit permission for any application installed outside Google Play. Because this APK is distributed directly to students, Android prompts for one-time approval.
What permissions does CalCredit use?
CalCredit requires standard Internet access to sync your GPA and academic profile with Firebase. It does not access your contacts, camera, SMS, files, or location.
Can I update the app later without losing data?
This APK can update the current direct-distributed build because it uses the same signing certificate. It is not a Play Store production signing key; installs from other signing identities cannot be updated in place.

iPhone Installation FAQ

Do I need an Apple Developer account or App Store payment?
No! CalCredit uses Apple's official Progressive Web App (PWA) standard. It is 100% free and does not require Apple IDs, credit cards, or App Store downloads.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once installed to your Home Screen, your academic regulations, courses, and calculated GPAs remain accessible on your device.
How do I get future updates?
Unlike normal App Store apps that require manual updates, CalCredit updates automatically every time you launch it with an active internet connection.
