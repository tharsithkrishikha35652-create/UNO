Add CalCredit to your iPhone

Install straight from Safari as a full-screen iPhone Web App with zero App Store fee.

  1. Tap Share in Safari

    Tap the Share icon in Safari’s toolbar (bottom bar on iPhone, top right on iPad) right on this page or after tapping Open in Safari.

  2. Select Add to Home Screen

    Scroll down the Apple share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.

  3. Keep Open as Web App on

    Leave Open as Web App switched on, then tap Add so CalCredit opens full screen on your iPhone Home Screen.

Does installing on iPhone cost anything?
No. CalCredit installs directly from Safari as an Apple Web App at zero cost, with no App Store purchase required.

Keeping Open as Web App enabled launches CalCredit full screen in its own app window instead of a browser tab.
